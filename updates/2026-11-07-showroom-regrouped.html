The Showroom work as one product

2026-11-07

My work at SparkVision used to sit here as nine separate projects. It is now one product page, Spark Digital Showroom, with its features, who built what, and the decisions that span it. The backend, the web front end, the desktop apps and the services that deploy and watch them are its parts.

Two other changes. The project pages no longer carry trade-offs and what I would change, since most of these projects are still being developed. And there is a new featured project from my first customer job: the event backend for Mimbly's water-recycling dashboard.
