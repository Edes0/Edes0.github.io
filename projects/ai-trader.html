Summary

A harness for backtesting and paper-trading stock strategies, where rule-based, machine-learning and LLM strategies plug into one interface.

What I did

  • Built a backtest engine with fill models, journaling, walk-forward runs and cross-validation, and about 22 strategies on top of it.
  • Built a scoring and review layer with rule-based, machine-learning, LLM and adversarial reviewers.
  • Built a live paper-trading loop with risk checks and reporting, covered by about 550 tests.

Outcomes

  • A running paper-trading setup with journaled research results; it has never traded real money.
