Summary

Jackon makes insulation. People who build houses and other buildings order it on Jackon's website, and the ordering system carries each order through the company until the people who pack it can see what to pack. At FrontEdge IT, alongside my first customer project and until I left, I maintained the website, the ordering system and the tools around them, and built new features for them.

What I did

  • Maintained Jackon's website, its ordering system and the tools around them, and built new features for them.
  • Built the algorithms that work out the most efficient way to pack an order: from the sizes ordered, which pallets to use and how many pieces fit on each.
