Summary

The Python service that segments a shopper's room photo into surfaces for the configurator's Bring Your Own Room feature and exports the shading the renderer needs, so shoppers can see products in their own room rather than a stock one. I added the asset exporter, per-pass timing and training-data retention to the existing service.

What I did

  • Tested whether the configurator's renderer could draw a dynamically detected surface, and built an asset exporter from the photo's shading with a versioned contract for the backend.
  • Added per-step timing that shows the CPU time, the device used and the component each step belongs to, so the slow parts of a 40-second job are visible before speeding them up.
  • Made the service keep every job as training data before purging it, and serve each job's training files to the backend's archive.
