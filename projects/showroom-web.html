Summary

The web front end of Spark Digital Showroom, SparkVision's room configurator, and the shopper-facing side of its rendering and API backend: a shopper picks a room, applies tiles, floors and furniture, and leaves with a summary sent to a shop or by mail.

What I did

  • Added front-end support for new tile patterns and shapes: offset patterns, hexagons and triangles, and later rotation at any angle and a grout-thickness slider.
  • Added Google Tag Manager tracking and mail-consent checkboxes, and made consent texts follow the active account with their own section for each summary exit.
  • Built the summary exits: mailing the summary and PDF to the shopper from the exit cards, a lead-capture mode, a Send in Shop card with a searchable salesperson list, and showing where the summary copy went.
  • Merged the model selector into the front end.
  • Built the phone remote-control flow for in-store displays.
  • Built the Bring Your Own Room screens: the add-your-room card and modal, a loading view for the shopper's room, hover states on surfaces and an apply-to-all row.
  • Introduced Playwright end-to-end tests, including cross-browser runs that caught a Firefox bug, and automated how new tests are created.
  • Added an agent guide, a glossary and a gotchas log to the repository.

Outcomes

  • The front end serves about 95 production showroom apps, about 70 of them with outside traffic in a given month.
