Summary

A replacement for the internal deploy service, which I am building from scratch. The old service could only redeploy; the new one gives a modern interface to deploy, uninstall, pause, restart and publish the applications on the production servers, so nobody has to log in over remote desktop for those jobs.

What I did

  • Specified the new service: a spec refined question by question and hardened over four review rounds, an architecture decision for one peer service per server, and 17 approved slices.
  • Built the new service behind the login on both web servers, chose the overview layout from a prototype, and built the overview of one server's applications.

Outcomes

  • Still under development; it will soon replace the old deploy service for internal use.
