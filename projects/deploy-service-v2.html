Summary

A replacement for the internal deploy service. The old service could only redeploy; the new one gives a modern interface to deploy, uninstall, pause, restart and publish the applications on the production servers.

What I did

  • Specified the new service: a spec refined question by question and hardened over four review rounds, an architecture decision for one peer service per server, and 17 approved slices.
  • Built the new service behind the login on both web servers, chose the overview layout from a prototype, and built the overview of one server's applications.
  • Extended the overview to both servers, with drift and attention signals, every deployable application and the deploys still running through the old service.
  • Built deploy and recycle of one application on one server with a live log, and a job queue per application family that refuses duplicates and cancels a running job for real.
  • Built a first end-to-end slice of publishing from stage to live, at night or at once.
  • Kept secrets out of the service's job log.
