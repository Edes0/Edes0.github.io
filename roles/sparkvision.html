Summary

SparkVision makes Spark Digital Showroom, a room configurator that tile, bathroom and floor retailers and manufacturers run on their websites and in-store screens, so shoppers can see products in a room before they buy.

I joined SparkVision in August 2023 and started on Spark Digital Showroom's web front end and model selector, and the .NET backend that renders the configured rooms and builds summaries.

I have always worked on these applications alone. In my first year a senior developer reviewed much of my work; since then I have been responsible for all of them, and I bring him in only for sensitive systems or larger architectural changes.

I started on site and moved to fully remote work after my first year.

In 2024 I worked on the Windows desktop app that hosts the configurator, moving it to .NET 8 and onto the Jenkins build, and in 2026 I rewrote it as a WPF application.

From May 2026 most of my work ran through Claude Code: I built a personal agent harness of skills, hooks and specs, and brought agent guides and workflows into the team's repositories.
