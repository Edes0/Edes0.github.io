About me

I am a software developer in Sweden, in production since 2022. I started at FrontEdge IT, where I got in through its recruitment test, built the backend of my first customer project and was the team's contact with the customer. Since 2023 I have worked at SparkVision on Spark Digital Showroom, a room configurator that runs as about 95 production apps, and since my second year there I have been responsible for it alone.

I like owning a system end to end, from the backend to the screen a shopper or a colleague uses, and staying with it after it ships. Since 2026 AI coding agents write most of my code: I cut the work into slices, make the design decisions and review every change, and I built the harness I work through and a shared workspace my colleagues use.

What I want next is nice colleagues: people I enjoy spending time with, at work and outside it, and something meaningful we build on together. Most of my time at SparkVision I have built alone.

In my own time I am building Autonomy, a programmable game where players write bots that play around the clock in one shared world, and I hope to launch it.
