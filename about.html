About me

I am a software developer in Sweden. Since 2023 I have worked on Spark Digital Showroom, a room configurator, and since 2026 much of my work runs through AI coding agents and the harness I built around them.

I like building systems end to end, from the backend to the tools other people use. In my own time I am building Autonomy, a programmable game where players write bots that play around the clock in one shared world, and I hope to launch it.
